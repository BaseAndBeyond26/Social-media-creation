# Product Marketing Context: Base & Beyond

*Last updated: 2026-10-05 (v1, pre-launch)*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Roof tent hire on the doorstep of the Lake District. Bring your own car
and we'll fit a premium roof tent so you can explore the National Park your way.
**Status:** Pre-launch. Bookings open soon; first available date **25 March 2027**
(Easter holidays). Content now builds an audience for launch.
**Business model:** Hire only (no tent sales). Customers book online (Booqable, with
prices shown on the booking form), then visit our unit to have the tent and roof
bars fitted and pick up extras.
**Product line:**
- **Moby Mountain** roof tents: the main, premium range.
- **TentBox** tents, marketed as our **lightweight** range for smaller cars whose
  roofs can't take the weight of bigger tents.
- **Pet-friendly tents:** dogs welcome.
- Roof bars fitted as needed.
- Extras, subject to availability at booking: bedding, camping equipment, paddle boards.
- Optional **custom trip itinerary** or local hotspot recommendations.
- **Welcome pack** with every hire.

## Location
Setmurthy, at the north end of **Bassenthwaite Lake**, just outside Cockermouth,
inside the **Lake District National Park** (a UNESCO World Heritage Site).
The location is our headline selling point: push it in the bio, captions and posts.
**No delivery:** customers collect from and return to our unit.

## Target Audience
- **Who:** couples and families, roughly 25-45.
- Mostly **first-timers**, plus **try-before-you-buy** people weighing up a roof tent purchase.
- Dog owners (pet-friendly tents).
- **Primary use case:** a Lake District adventure without booking hotels or buying
  kit, using the car they already own.

## Problems & Pain Points
- Never tried roof-tent camping; unsure where to start or which kit is needed.
- Roof tents are expensive to buy, so they want to try one first.
- Hotels and cottages in the Lakes are pricey and book up; tent camping on the ground is a faff.
- Want to bring the dog.

## Objections (and our answers)
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| The Lake District weather is notoriously wet | Off-ground, weatherproof tent; cosy bedding; we plan wet-day options into itineraries. Lean into it with humour. |
| Will it fit my car? | We check your car details at booking and only offer tents that fit (TentBox lightweight range for smaller cars). |
| How much is it? | Prices are shown upfront on the booking form; there are no surprises. |
| It's a long drive to the Lakes | Make the drive part of the adventure; recommend stopovers; longer hires make the trip worth it. |
| Is it hard to set up? | We fit it and show you how. *(Film real setup times with the on-screen timer.)* |

## Competitive Landscape
- **Hotels, B&Bs and cottages:** comfortable but pricey, fixed in one place, and book up.
- **Campervan hire:** expensive, a big vehicle on narrow Lakes roads, and you drive their van rather than your own car.
- **Ground tents and campsites:** cheap but cold, wet and slow to set up.
- **Other roof tent hire firms:** may not be based in the National Park itself or include the personal touches.

## Differentiation
1. **Location:** we're inside the National Park, so the adventure starts the moment you leave our unit.
2. **Personal touch:** welcome packs, custom itineraries, local knowledge.
3. **Premium kit:** Moby Mountain tents, plus a lightweight range so smaller cars aren't left out.
4. **Pet friendly.**

## Brand Voice
- **Tone:** friendly and down to earth, like a local mate who knows the best spots.
- **Personality:** cheeky moments and humour where they fit (especially weather jokes).
- **Values:** love for the Lake District. Respect the countryside, leave it better than you
  found it, pick up litter, and park and camp responsibly. This is a core content pillar.
- **Avoid:** corporate or salesy tone, hype and exaggeration, AI-sounding phrasing,
  and implying a TentBox or Moby partnership unless one exists.

## Content Guardrails
- **Never imply customers can wild camp or sleep anywhere.** In England (outside
  Dartmoor) wild camping generally needs landowner permission, and many Lake District
  car parks don't allow overnight stays. Show campsites and permitted sites, and frame
  "spots" responsibly.
- No fake reviews or invented stats while pre-launch. Don't claim "most popular" or
  "best-rated" until it's true.
- "England's most beautiful national park" is fine as an opinion ("arguably").
- Show number plates only with approval.
